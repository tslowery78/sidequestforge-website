Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Our apps collect nothing about you, and we cannot see what you do in them. This page explains, app by app, what stays on your device, what goes to Apple, and the little we receive: only email you choose to send us.

The short version

  • None of our apps have ads, analytics, tracking, crash reporting or accounts.
  • Your game data stays on your device and, for some apps, in your own iCloud. We cannot see it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or Apple ID.
  • We only get your email address if you write to us or sign up for app news on this website.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

PlateTag66

SideQuest Forge does not collect any information from PlateTag66.

  • No accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • Trips, plates and settings are saved on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud Drive so they follow you to your other devices. We cannot see them.
  • Location is optional and only used while the app is open. If you allow it, the app adds a location to each plate you log, at the precision you pick in Settings: precise, approximate, city only, or off.
  • Saved locations are encrypted on your device and in your iCloud Drive, and are deleted automatically after the time you set in Settings (one year by default). We never receive them.
  • To show place names, the app sends coordinates or your place search to Apple's map service, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Settings has Export My Data and Clear All Game Data.
  • Any purchase is optional and handled by Apple. We never see payment details.

Color Dots

Color Dots does not collect any information.

  • No accounts, names, emails or photos.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • Coloring works fully offline. The only connection is to Apple's App Store, for the optional All Pictures unlock. Buying or restoring is behind a grown-ups check.
  • Coloring progress and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Color Dots is made for children. Because it collects nothing, there is nothing to share, sell or delete. Questions: support@sidequestforgestudio.com

Lumberjack Jack

SideQuest Forge does not collect any information from Lumberjack Jack.

  • No accounts, names, emails or photos.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • Game progress and settings are saved on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account so they follow you to your other devices. We cannot see them.
  • Game Center is optional. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, your Endless scores go to Apple's Game Center leaderboard automatically, under Apple's privacy policy. To stop this, sign out of Game Center in iPhone Settings.
  • The Full Trail purchase is handled by Apple. We never see payment details.

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share, sell or delete. Questions: support@sidequestforgestudio.com

Spud HUD

SideQuest Forge does not collect any information from Spud HUD.

PipPoint

SideQuest Forge does not collect any information from PipPoint.

  • No accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • Goals, check-ins, optional questionnaire answers, journal, discoveries and settings are saved on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We cannot see them.
  • Widgets and the Apple Watch app show and sync your information between your own devices.
  • Reminders are local notifications, only with your permission. PipPoint does not read your contacts or calendar.
  • If you export or share, you choose where it goes; that place's privacy rules apply to your copy.
  • Resetting the app clears current progress. Older device or iCloud backups may remain until you remove them.
  • The optional wellness questionnaires are for information only. They do not diagnose or replace professional care.

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share, sell or delete. Questions: support@sidequestforgestudio.com

This website

  • No ads, analytics or tracking cookies. The site only remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. To deliver the page, they receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies.
  • If you sign up for app news, the form sends your email address to Formspree, which passes it on to us.

Email you send us

If you email us or sign up for app news, we get your email address and anything you write. We use it only to answer you, or to send the occasional app news you asked for. Only the studio owner reads it. We keep it until you ask us to delete it.

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, or to stop app news, email support@sidequestforgestudio.com.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. We would only hand it over if the law required it.

Services we use

  • Apple: App Store, in-app purchases, iCloud, Game Center and map place names.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts: fonts on this website.
  • Formspree: the app news sign-up forms on this website.

Each has its own privacy policy.

Children

Color Dots is made for children and collects nothing. None of our apps collect personal information from anyone, including children. The sign-up forms on this website are for grown-ups. If a child has sent us their email address, write to support@sidequestforgestudio.com and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Deleting an app deletes its data on your device. Data in your iCloud can be removed in iPhone Settings under your name, then iCloud.
  • Turn location on or off for PlateTag66 at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete anything you sent us.

Where data is handled

We are in the United States. Email and website sign-ups are handled in the United States.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
