❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What devices are supported?

PlateTag 66 is available for iPhone and iPad devices running iOS 17.0 or later.

Does it work offline?

Yes! PlateTag 66 works perfectly without an internet connection, so you can play anywhere your adventures take you.

Is it suitable for young children?

Absolutely! The app is designed for all ages with intuitive controls and family-friendly content that everyone can enjoy.

How does GPS tracking work?

The app uses your device's GPS to automatically detect your location and suggest nearby states. GPS usage is optional and can be disabled in settings.

Can I use it for multiple trips?

Free users can create one trip. The optional one-time Pro upgrade unlocks unlimited trip tracking for families who want to organize every adventure.

What's included in PlateTag 66 Pro?

PlateTag 66 Pro is a one-time purchase that removes banner ads and unlocks unlimited trip tracking. There are no subscriptions or recurring fees.

How do I restore Pro?

Open the app menu and tap Restore Purchases. Restores use the same Apple ID that made the original App Store purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are handled through Apple's App Store refund policy. Contact Apple Support directly for refund assistance.
